## Information stored on your device

Query Quest saves game progress and settings on your device. This can include completed and unlocked levels, XP, coins, inventory, hints, badges, name cards, your display name, and your selected profile picture. The profile picture is stored on the device and is not included in the app's optional progress sync.

SQL practice queries are evaluated in the app using its sample data. They are not sent to a live database or uploaded as part of progress sync.

You can remove locally stored app data through your Android device's app settings, or by uninstalling the app. Depending on Android's backup settings, device-level backup behavior may also apply.

## Optional cloud progress sync

Cloud sync is optional and must be enabled when the app is built. **The current Query Quest APK release does not have Supabase sync configured; its gameplay data stays on the device.**

If a future or separately configured build enables sync, Query Quest creates an anonymous Supabase account and may send a player ID and game progress/profile fields, including level progress, XP, coins, inventory, display name, equipped badge, name card, and update time. The app does not send your SQL queries or profile picture through this sync. No email address or password is requested for anonymous sync.

Supabase processes synced information to provide this feature. Its handling of service infrastructure is also subject to Supabase's applicable terms and privacy materials. Synced records may remain in the configured Supabase project until the app operator deletes them. To ask about deletion of synced progress, contact **[contact email]** and include your in-game display name and, if available, player ID. Do not send passwords or security codes.

## Photos

If you choose a profile picture, Android's photo picker lets you select an image. The selected image is kept in the app's device storage for display in your profile. The current app does not upload that image.

## Sharing and advertising

The current app is designed for single-player practice. It does not include in-app advertising or third-party analytics integration. Information may be processed by your device and operating-system providers under their own services and settings. If optional Supabase sync is enabled in a future build, the progress fields described above are sent to the Supabase project configured by the app operator.

## Security and retention

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. We use the storage and access controls provided by Android and, when configured, Supabase. No internet-based service can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to select a profile picture. In the current release, you can clear local app data through Android settings. If cloud sync is enabled, you may request access, correction, or deletion of synced progress by emailing **[contact email]**. We may need enough information to identify the relevant game record, but do not send sensitive credentials.
